Developed a multimodal emotion recognition system combining visual and audio signals for affective computing. The project explores cross-modal fusion strategies and applied machine learning for robust emotion recognition.

### Key Details

- Integrated ResNet18, WavLM, and cross-attention fusion for joint audio-visual representation learning
- Conducted comparative experiments across late fusion, concat fusion, gated fusion, and cross-attention fusion
- Improved model robustness and training stability through two-stage fine-tuning and noise-aware training
- Academic project (Aug 2025 – Feb 2026)

### Technologies

PyTorch, ResNet18, WavLM, cross-attention, multimodal learning, audio-visual fusion

### Status

Academic project. Manuscript in progress (multimodal emotion supervision system).
