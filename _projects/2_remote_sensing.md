Explored research ideas connecting satellite imagery, climate variables, geospatial coordinates, and generative modeling. The work focuses on how environmental and spatial information can be integrated into AI models for city-scale analysis.

### Key Details

- Studied satellite image resolution and climate data resolution mismatch
- Investigated external climate data sources and coordinate-based data querying
- Focused on linking visual remote-sensing data with climate and environmental variables

### Technologies

Remote sensing, geospatial data, climate data, generative AI, Python, data processing
