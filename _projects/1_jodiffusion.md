Constructed a multimodal generative framework integrating satellite imagery and urban energy data using diffusion-based models and ControlNet conditioning. The project investigates whether synthetic data generated by diffusion models can improve downstream semantic segmentation under limited real-data regimes.

### Key Details

- Built and processed large-scale multi-city datasets (MUSE), aligning satellite images with energy consumption labels
- Evaluated synthetic-only, real-only, and mixed-data training settings for semantic segmentation
- Conducted downstream experiments demonstrating enhanced segmentation performance (mIoU) under limited real-data regimes
- Contributed to model design, ablation studies, and experimental validation of generative data augmentation strategies
- Used MMSegmentation, Mask2Former, PyTorch, Hugging Face tooling, W&B, and multi-GPU training

### Technologies

PyTorch, Diffusion Models, ControlNet, Hugging Face, MMSegmentation, Mask2Former, OpenMMLab, W&B, Linux, multi-GPU training
